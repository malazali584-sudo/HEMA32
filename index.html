🔬 Practical Laboratory

Hematology Practical Labs

Lab 1: Red Blood Cell Count (RBC Count)

Aim:

To determine the number of red blood cells in a blood sample.

Principle:

Red blood cells are diluted using an appropriate diluting fluid and counted under the microscope using a haemocytometer.

Materials:

Blood sample, RBC pipette or micropipette, diluting fluid, haemocytometer, coverslip and microscope.

Method:

The blood sample is mixed with the appropriate diluting fluid. The diluted sample is introduced into the haemocytometer and red blood cells are counted under the microscope.

Result:

The RBC count is expressed as cells per microlitre (cells/µL).
